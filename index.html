Maybe you’re here because...

The ways you’ve always managed things no longer seem to work. Perhaps you’re struggling with anxiety, difficult emotions, relationship challenges, or a major life change. You may feel stuck, disconnected, or simply sense that something is missing. Therapy offers a space to slow down, understand yourself more deeply, and explore new possibilities for a life that feels more meaningful, connected, and alive.

Ready to take the first step?

I believe meaningful change often begins when we feel genuinely met with compassion and curiosity. My approach is relational, somatic, and trauma-informed, grounded in warmth, openness, and attentive presence. Together, we’ll create space to explore what matters to you, at a pace that feels right. There’s no fixed formula. We’ll follow what emerges, with trust in your capacity for healing, growth, and a more authentic way of being.
