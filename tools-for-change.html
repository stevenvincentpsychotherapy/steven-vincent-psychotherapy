Tools for Change

Meaningful change doesn't happen only in the therapy room. While our sessions offer a space for exploration, insight, and connection, much of the work of healing and growth unfolds in everyday life. Therapy is something we do together, but the possibilities for change extend far beyond our sessions.

I believe in helping people make the most of their investment in themselves. Part of my approach involves supporting you in developing practical tools, deepening self-awareness, and discovering new ways of relating to yourself and the world around you.

Drawing from a range of approaches, from evidence-informed health science to the wisdom of contemplative traditions, we can explore practices and ways of being that support personal growth and deepen your therapeutic process between sessions.

Together, we'll explore new possibilities and discover what works for you, developing practices that align with your values, respond to your needs, and feel meaningful and sustainable. The intention is to help you cultivate ways of supporting yourself that become a natural part of how you live, grow, and meet life's challenges.
