Tools for Change

We live at a remarkable time, with access to an extraordinary wealth of knowledge about human growth, healing, and well-being. From advances in psychology, neuroscience, and health science to the wisdom of contemplative traditions, there are many ways to deepen our understanding of ourselves and discover new possibilities for change.

Over the years, I've explored a wide range of approaches to personal growth and healing, drawing from contemporary psychology, somatic and contemplative practices, depth psychology, and wisdom traditions. I'm particularly interested in how these different perspectives can complement one another, offering new ways to understand ourselves and support meaningful change.

This might involve developing greater emotional awareness and self-regulation, reconnecting with the body through movement and mindful attention, exploring dreams and deeper psychological patterns, or cultivating everyday practices that support well-being, meaningful connection, and a more fulfilling life.

I believe meaningful change doesn't happen only in the therapy room. While our sessions offer a space for exploration, insight, and connection, much of the work of healing and growth unfolds in everyday life. Part of my approach is helping you develop practical tools and ways of supporting yourself that can deepen and enrich your therapeutic process between sessions.

Together, we can draw from this wealth of possibilities to discover what genuinely works for you. Rather than prescribing a set of exercises, we'll explore practices that respond to your needs, align with your values, and feel meaningful and sustainable. My hope is to help you develop ways of caring for yourself, navigating challenges, and continuing to grow that become a natural part of your everyday life.
